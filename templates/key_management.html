🔄 Key Management Workflow


1. ML-KEM-768 generates a key pair.
2. ML-KEM-768 establishes a shared secret.
3. HKDF-SHA3-256 derives the AES-256 encryption key.
4. ML-DSA-65 generates and verifies digital signatures.
